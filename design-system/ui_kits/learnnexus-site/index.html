Built for completion

Everything after “here’s the course link”.

Most platforms stop at hosting content. The hard part is getting 1,200 people through it before the deadline — and proving it afterwards.

Rule-based enrollment

Assign by org unit, role or hire date. New joiners are enrolled the day they appear in your directory — no spreadsheet, no chasing.

Manager-scoped reporting

Every manager sees their own subtree and nothing above it, resolved from a materialised org path — so the numbers are right without a permissions audit.

Verifiable certificates

Serial-numbered, expiry-aware and publicly verifiable by code. Revoke one and the verification page says so immediately.

Blended delivery

Self-paced modules, live sessions with attendance, and classroom cohorts — tracked on one progress record instead of three systems.

Audit trail that holds up

Append-only logs enforced at the database, not just the app layer. Who enrolled whom, who graded what, and exactly when.

Your brand, your domain

Custom domain, logo and a full colour theme from three values. Learners never see ours.
